🆕 New in 1.5.0 — Round 1: New & Now
  • NEW + version pills — fresh tiles wear a NEW badge for two weeks; tiles with a version in their notes show it as a pill
  • Weather heads-up — the strip warns you about tomorrow: rain ≥50%, a freeze, or 90°+ heat

  • Search count, tile details, share-the-deck, install tip — results show their count, long-press shows a tile’s site, and the deck itself is shareable from the footer

  • Long-press a tile — hold any tile for a quick-actions sheet: Open, Favorite, Copy link, Share.
  • Recent searches — your last 4 searches sit under the search box for one-tap repeats; press / anywhere to jump to search.
  • Roulette feels real — Surprise Me now eases out like a wheel slowing down, and it picks from your current filter instead of resetting it.

  • New Muse tools — 🎨 Accent cycler and 🧹 Reset data join the widget (25 tools total).
